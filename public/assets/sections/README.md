# Section illustrations

These six AI-generated editorial photographs illustrate learning areas and study
activities. They do not depict actual G WORLD students, staff, campuses or
facilities and are not endorsements or accreditation evidence.

- `management.webp`: a management/financial decision-making seminar.
- `art.webp`: creative communication in a recording studio.
- `science.webp`: microscopy in a biology teaching laboratory.
- `python.webp`: hands-on programming at a laptop.
- `study.webp`: an overhead study planner and revision materials.
- `project.webp`: research notes and reference books.

All were generated for G WORLD, then resized and encoded to WebP. The six
compositions are deliberately distinct from each other and the existing hero
and welcome photos. Original large PNG intermediates are not retained.
Faculty covers are shared between the homepage showcase and directory to keep
the same faculty recognisable; they are not reused as resource-story images or
as the separate vector subpage hero illustrations.
